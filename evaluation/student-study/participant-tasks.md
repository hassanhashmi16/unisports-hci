# UniSports student walkthrough

UniSports is a classroom prototype for campus tournaments. Use the fictional names and roll numbers on these cards. The facilitator will give you one card at a time. As you work, say what you are looking for and what you expect to happen. You can pause, skip a task, or stop at any time.

## A. Enter a badminton pair

You are helping Mariam and Adeel enter mixed doubles. Mariam and Adeel are both enrolled at FAST Lahore. Find out whether they qualify, when registration closes, and what the pair will pay. Enter the pair and reopen their receipt. Explain whether they are cleared to play and what they should do next.
